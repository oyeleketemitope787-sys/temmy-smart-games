Test statements about adding and subtracting odd and even numbers. Use examples to help you decide, then look for a counterexample when a statement is not always true.

Remember
Even + even = even
Odd + odd = even
Odd + even = odd
For subtraction
Even − even = even
Odd − odd = even
Odd − even = odd
Even − odd = odd (when the result is a whole number).

A few examples can support a statement, but a counterexample is enough to show that an “always true” statement is false.
